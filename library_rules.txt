LIBRARY HOURS
The library is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Saturday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Sunday. The library is closed on major holidays including Christmas, New Year's Day, and Thanksgiving.

BORROWING LIMITS
Students can borrow up to 3 books at a time. The loan period is 14 days from the borrow date. Books can be renewed once for an additional 7 days if no other student has placed a hold on them.

LATE FEES
Late returns incur a fee of $1 per day per book. Fees are calculated from the due date until the book is returned. Maximum late fee per book is $30.

BOOK CONDITIONS
Books must be returned in good condition. Lost books require replacement cost plus a $5 processing fee. Damaged books will be assessed on a case-by-case basis by the librarian.

RETURN PROCESS
To return a book, submit a return request through your dashboard. A librarian will inspect the book and approve the return within 1-2 business days. After approval, the book is removed from your borrowed list.

RECOMMENDATIONS
The library has books across various genres including Fantasy, Science Fiction, Mystery, Romance, Thriller, Horror, Biography, History, and Non-fiction. You can ask for personalized recommendations based on your reading preferences.

CONTACT INFORMATION
For questions, contact the library at library@example.com or call (555) 123-4567. You can also visit the library in person during business hours.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
Students must keep their library cards safe, report lost books immediately, and respect all library rules. No food or drinks are allowed near books.
